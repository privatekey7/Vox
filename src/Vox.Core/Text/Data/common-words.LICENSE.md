# common-words.txt.gz

The 48 217 most frequent Cyrillic word forms of `ru_50k.txt` from
[hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) (content/2018/ru), built from
OpenSubtitles. Words only, frequencies dropped, gzip-compressed.

self-learning never turns an ordinary Russian word into a replacement.
